Market Sound

Hear any Nifty 50 trading day as a tone, an Om drone or a beat. Is it a still lake or a hurricane? The price shapes the pitch and the size of each move sets the loudness. An analyzer then calls the next beat and the next tone.

buyers pushing sellers pushing standoff

    Every trade has a buyer and a seller. The commentary reads price and volume to guess which side is pushing harder. It cannot see real orders, so treat it as colour, not information.

    Beat drops and what comes next

    A beat drop is a minute where the price moves at least three times its normal size. They are marked on the chart. Before every minute the analyzer makes two calls: will the next beat be louder or quieter than usual, and will the next tone go up or down.

    What the sound is made of

    -1.83slope of the price spectrum. A pure random walk is -2, a low rumble.
    -0.02slope of the minute-to-minute changes. Flat is white noise, a hiss.
    2,328full sessions measured, April 2017 to October 2026.

    Across those sessions, Nifty's minute prices carry about the same mix of low and high frequencies as a random walk. Real days also bunch their big moves together, which a shuffle spreads out, and that is one thing your ear may pick up. The mapping to sound is our choice. It makes the shape of a day audible, and it does not reveal what traders are thinking.
